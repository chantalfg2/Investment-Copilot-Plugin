# Trusted Sources — Investment Research

Whitelist of sources for the monthly brief. Skill should query these for primary data, secondary commentary, and analyst consensus. Avoid sources outside this list unless explicitly justified in the brief.

---

## Tier 1 — Primary Financial Data & News

| Source | Use For | URL |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Bloomberg** | Market data, equity research summaries, macro analysis | bloomberg.com |
| **Reuters** | Breaking financial news, geopolitics, corporate actions | reuters.com |
| **Financial Times (FT)** | Macro and policy analysis (esp. global) | ft.com |
| **Wall Street Journal (WSJ)** | US-centric macro, policy, corporate news | wsj.com |
| **Yahoo Finance** | Free quotes, ETF profiles, analyst consensus, news aggregation | finance.yahoo.com |
| **Morningstar** | ETF/mutual fund ratings (gold/silver/bronze), star ratings, fund analyst reports | morningstar.com |

## Tier 2 — Analyst Coverage & Ratings

| Source | Use For | URL |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **S&P Global Market Intelligence** | Sector reports, credit research | spglobal.com |
| **Argus Research** | Independent equity research | argusresearch.com |
| **Zacks Investment Research** | Earnings revisions, ranking system | zacks.com |
| **Seeking Alpha (Pro tier articles only)** | Analyst deep dives — exclude retail-only contributor articles | seekingalpha.com |
| **Schwab/Fidelity research portals** | Aggregate analyst consensus | schwab.com, fidelity.com |

## Tier 3 — Macro / Policy Sources

| Source | Use For | URL |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Federal Reserve** | FOMC statements, minutes, dot plot, policy speeches | federalreserve.gov |
| **Bureau of Labor Statistics** | CPI, PPI, employment data | bls.gov |
| **Bureau of Economic Analysis** | GDP, personal income/spending | bea.gov |
| **IMF** | Global growth forecasts, country reports | imf.org |
| **OECD** | Cross-country economic indicators | oecd.org |
| **CBO** | US fiscal projections, policy scoring | cbo.gov |
| **Tax Foundation, Brookings, Peterson Institute** | Policy analysis (use with awareness of think-tank slant) | various |

## Tier 4 — Sector Specialists (consult when sector is in focus)

| Sector | Specialist Sources |
|--------|---------------------|
| Energy | EIA, IEA, BloombergNEF |
| Healthcare | FDA, BioPharma Catalyst, Endpoints News |
| Financials | FDIC, OCC, Fed bank stress test results |
| Defense | Janes, CSIS, DoD budget |
| Real Estate | NAREIT, S&P Case-Shiller, Federal Reserve Z.1 |
| International | Country-specific central banks, MSCI factsheets |

---

## Sources to Avoid

- **Reddit / r/wallstreetbets / retail forums** — not research-grade
- **Twitter/X** — except verified accounts of analysts/journalists, and only as secondary
- **Sponsored content / promotional newsletters** — bias risk
- **YouTube finance influencers** — not research-grade
- **Crypto-specific sources** unless the user explicitly opts in (default exclude — not in current investment thesis)

---

## Citation Format

Every claim in the brief cites:
- Source name
- Article/report title (or instrument page)
- URL
- Retrieval date (YYYY-MM-DD)

Example:
> *Bloomberg, "Fed Holds Rates Steady; Powell Cites Sticky Inflation," May 2, 2026, bloomberg.com/news/articles/... (retrieved 2026-05-03)*

If a Tier 1 source is paywalled and not accessible via available tools, fall back to Tier 2 with a note: "(Bloomberg paywall; cross-referenced with Yahoo Finance and Morningstar)".

---

## Refresh Cadence

- **Macro data**: must be ≤7 days old
- **Earnings/analyst ratings**: ≤30 days old, or flag as stale
- **Holdings/AUM/expense ratio**: ≤90 days old (these change slowly)
- **Forward guidance**: most recent quarterly update
