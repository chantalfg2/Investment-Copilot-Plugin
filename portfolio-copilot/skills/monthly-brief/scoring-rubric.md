# Scoring Rubric — Investment Picks

Each candidate is scored 1–5 on six dimensions. Total max = 30. Top 5 by total advance to the brief. Tiebreaker: **fit-to-portfolio**, then **macro tailwinds**, then **analyst consensus**.

---

## 1. Fit-to-Portfolio (1–5)

How well does the pick close a stated gap in `portfolio-context.md`?

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Closes a major gap (sector or geo) where the user is materially under-exposed |
| 4 | Closes a moderate gap, or adds diversification within an under-weighted dimension |
| 3 | Adds modest diversification; neither closes a gap nor duplicates significantly |
| 2 | Some overlap with existing holdings; fit is marginal |
| 1 | Duplicates existing exposure or adds to an over-weighted area (e.g., more US tech) |

---

## 2. Growth Potential (1–5)

Forward 3–5 year return expectation based on consensus estimates and secular trends.

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Top-quartile growth: secular tailwinds, accelerating earnings/revenue, strong consensus upgrades |
| 4 | Above-average growth: solid forward trajectory, supportive secular trend |
| 3 | Average growth: consistent with broad market or sector benchmark |
| 2 | Below-average forward expectations or visible deceleration |
| 1 | Declining fundamentals or structurally challenged |

---

## 3. Risk Profile (1–5)

Higher score = better fit for "moderate-to-aggressive growth." Note: this rewards calibrated risk, not minimum risk.

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Aggressive but well-diversified ETF or high-conviction stock with asymmetric upside; volatility justified |
| 4 | Moderate-aggressive; volatility in expected range; drawdown history acceptable |
| 3 | Moderate; standard market risk |
| 2 | Conservative — fine for ballast but doesn't match growth thesis |
| 1 | Either too conservative for stated risk profile, or speculative beyond "aggressive" (single-name biotech with binary catalysts, leveraged ETFs, etc.) |

---

## 4. Valuation (1–5)

Quality of current entry point.

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Materially below historical/peer valuation; clear margin of safety |
| 4 | Modestly attractive vs. peers/history |
| 3 | Fair value; neither cheap nor expensive |
| 2 | Stretched valuation; entering near recent highs without commensurate growth |
| 1 | Significantly overvalued; risk/reward skewed against entry |

For ETFs, evaluate based on underlying basket P/E vs. category average and any premium/discount to NAV.

---

## 5. Macro Tailwinds (1–5)

Alignment with Phase 1 macro scan.

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Direct beneficiary of multiple macro tailwinds identified in scan |
| 4 | Aligns with one strong macro tailwind |
| 3 | Macro-neutral; neither helped nor hurt |
| 2 | Faces one identified macro headwind |
| 1 | Multiple macro headwinds; thesis fights the tape |

---

## 6. Analyst Consensus (1–5)

Recency and strength of independent analyst coverage. Sources: Morningstar, Yahoo Finance analyst page, Bloomberg consensus, Argus, S&P, equity research aggregators.

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 5 | Strong Buy / 4-5 star Morningstar / multiple recent upgrades; high coverage |
| 4 | Buy or Outperform consensus; coverage current within 30 days |
| 3 | Hold/Neutral consensus; mixed signals |
| 2 | Sell-leaning or recent downgrades; thin coverage |
| 1 | No coverage or strongly negative consensus |

---

## Application Notes

- **Be honest, not generous.** A 5 should be rare.
- **Justify each score in 1 sentence** in working notes (don't include in final brief unless space allows).
- **Flag low-confidence scores** when data is thin — note "(low confidence)" next to the score.
- **Disqualifying criteria** — regardless of total score, a candidate is dropped if:
  - Expense ratio >0.75% for an ETF without a unique exposure
  - Trailing 5Y return materially negative without a credible turnaround thesis
  - Holdings list duplicates >30% of an ETF the user already owns
  - Single-stock with binary near-term catalyst (FDA decision, lawsuit verdict) unless asymmetry is extreme
